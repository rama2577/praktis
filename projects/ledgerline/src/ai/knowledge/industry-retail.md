## Monthly Closing Checklist
1. Semua transaksi di-journal
2. Bank reconciliation
3. Stock opname
4. Penyusutan + amortisasi
5. Prepaid allocation
6. Accrued expenses
7. Revenue recognition (PSAK 72)
8. PPN + PPh reconciliation
9. Trial balance → financial statements
10. Tax computation → SPT preparation
